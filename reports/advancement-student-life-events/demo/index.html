Conference Breakout Data

A concept for showing interest and attendance across a conference's breakout sessions. Every number on this page is invented for layout review — nothing here is wired to Slate yet.

Interest
Selections by Breakout Session

How many registrants picked each breakout for its time slot, captured at registration. Each registrant picks one talk per slot.

Attendance
Check-ins by Breakout Session

Actual attendance at each breakout, with the share of interested registrants who showed up.

Breakout attendance totals more than overall conference attendance because most attendees take in a session in each time slot.
